the cat sat by the window. the sun was warm and the room was quiet. a small bird landed on the fence. the cat watched the bird, but the bird flew away.

the dog found a red ball in the garden. he took the ball to the gate. mia opened the gate and threw the ball. the dog ran across the grass and brought it back.

leo had a little boat made of wood. he placed it in the stream and watched it move. a leaf floated beside the boat. both went under the bridge and out of sight.

the rain began before lunch. mia closed the window and put the cups on the table. leo made some tea. they listened to the rain and read their books.

the bird built a nest in the old tree. every morning it carried a small twig to the branch. one day the nest was ready. the bird rested inside while the wind moved the leaves.

leo planted a seed in a brown pot. he gave it water and set it near the window. for several days nothing seemed to happen. then a tiny green shoot appeared above the soil.

mia lost a blue button on the way to the shop. she looked near the wall and under the bench. the dog sniffed the path. at last mia found the button beside a stone.

the old clock stopped at noon. leo looked at its hands and heard no sound. he turned the small key at the back. the clock began to tick again.

the cat hid inside an empty box. mia walked past the box without seeing him. then a soft tail moved over the edge. mia laughed and sat down beside the box.

the sun rose over the hill. the fields were pale with mist. a farmer opened the barn and carried a bucket to the yard. soon the whole village was awake.

mia wanted to draw the tree outside. she found a pencil and a clean sheet of paper. first she drew the trunk. then she added branches, leaves, and a bird on the highest branch.

leo made a shelf for his books. he measured each piece of wood before he cut it. the first shelf leaned to one side. he moved a support and tried again until it stood straight.

the dog heard a sound behind the door. he waited with his nose close to the floor. when the door opened, mia came in with a bag of apples. she put the bag on the table.

the children walked to the pond after breakfast. they saw three ducks near the reeds. the smallest duck followed the others across the water. a breeze made little waves behind them.

mia baked a loaf of bread. the kitchen grew warm as it cooked. when the bread was ready, she put it on a wooden board. leo cut a slice and spread it with butter.

the moon was bright above the garden. the cat moved along the wall and stopped near the gate. somewhere in the dark, a bird called once. the cat listened, then returned to the house.

leo found a map in an old book. the map showed a path through the woods. he followed the path with his finger. it ended at a small lake beside a tall hill.

mia filled a jar with smooth stones from the beach. one stone was gray, one was white, and one was almost black. she placed them in a line on the sand. the next wave washed the line away.

the little boat had a hole in its side. water slowly filled the bottom. leo pulled it from the stream and let it dry. he covered the hole and tested the boat in a bowl.

the wind blew a hat across the square. mia ran after it, but the hat moved faster. it rolled past the shop and stopped against a fence. mia picked it up and brushed away the dust.

the garden was dry after a week without rain. leo carried water from the tap. he gave a little to each plant. by evening the leaves looked firm again.

the cat and the dog shared a sunny step. the cat slept while the dog watched the road. a cart passed the house with a soft rattle. neither animal left the warm stone.

mia learned a new song on a small piano. at first she played it very slowly. she stopped at the same difficult part each time. after some practice, her hands moved through the notes without stopping.

leo counted the windows on the street. some were open and some were closed. one had a flower pot, and another had a sleeping cat. he wrote the number in his notebook.

the shop had a bell above its door. it rang when mia stepped inside. she bought flour, milk, and a green apple. on the way home, she heard the bell ring for the next visitor.

the bird dropped a feather near the bench. leo picked it up and held it against the light. its edges were soft and very thin. he left it on the bench for someone else to find.

mia and leo built a small tent in the garden. they used a sheet, two chairs, and a long piece of string. inside they read stories until it was time for supper.

the path was covered with yellow leaves. the dog ran ahead and made them scatter. mia walked slowly so she could hear the leaves under her feet. autumn had arrived in the village.

leo could not find his pencil. he checked the table, the shelf, and his coat. then he opened the book he had been reading. the pencil was there, keeping his place.

the snow fell all night. in the morning the garden looked new. mia saw a line of small tracks beside the fence. she followed them to the tree, where the bird was waiting.

the cat watched a drop of water fall from the tap. another drop followed, and then another. he touched the sink with one paw. the water was cold, so he stepped away.

mia carried a lamp to the little shed. leo was looking for a tool on the shelf. with the light he could see the back corner. the tool had fallen behind a wooden box.

the stream was higher after the rain. the little bridge stayed dry, but the stones below it disappeared. leo stood on the bank and watched a branch move quickly past.

mia put two cups on the table. she poured tea into one and warm milk into the other. the cat slept on the chair beside her. outside, the last light faded from the garden.

leo opened his notebook to a fresh page. he drew the boat he wanted to build next. this one would be wider and have a small sail. he wrote down the pieces he would need.

the dog waited at the gate each afternoon. he knew the sound of mia walking up the path. when he heard her steps, his tail began to move. soon they were together in the garden again.

the morning was quiet after the storm. mia opened the window and smelled the wet leaves. a bird sat on the fence with a twig in its beak. the garden had many small things to repair.

leo found the red ball under the bench. he rolled it toward the dog and watched it bounce over a stone. the dog caught it near the gate. they played until the sun moved behind the house.

mia took a book to the pond. she sat beneath the tree and read a few pages. a duck moved through the water, leaving a narrow trail. she looked up from the book and watched until the trail was gone.

the cat returned to the warm window. the room was quiet, and the clock was ticking. leo put away his pencil while mia closed her book. outside, a small bird settled in the old tree.
